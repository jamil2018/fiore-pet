# Code of Conduct

Purpose: set expectations for participation in Fiore's community.
last-verified: 2026-11-09

Be respectful, patient and constructive in issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code, not people; accept corrections and respect boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized content, doxxing, deliberate intimidation and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's report-content tools for abusive content. For a repository moderation request, contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/jamil2018) through an available contact method on their profile, or open an issue that asks for a private reporting channel without including sensitive details. Do not post private allegations or personal information in public issues.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Participants may ask for clarification or reconsideration without retaliation or harassment.

Use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md) for software vulnerabilities.
